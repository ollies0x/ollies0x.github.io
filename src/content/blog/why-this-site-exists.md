Every platform I post on is borrowed land.

X can change its algorithm tomorrow. LinkedIn can decide my reach is worth less than a promoted post. Google can reshuffle everything I rank for with one core update and a shrug. That's the deal with rented land - you build the house, someone else owns the dirt.

This site is the dirt I own.

## What this is

A build log. When I ship something at Underdog Digital, when an AI agent does something interesting on my stack, when I find something broken about how search works - it lands here. Long form, no character limit, no engagement score underneath it.

I run an SEO agency, so yes, I'm aware of the irony of launching a personal blog in 2026 and caring about backlinks. The difference is I get to show my working. Every experiment, every number, every thing that didn't work. Try getting that from an agency case study.

## What this is not

It's not a diary. You'll get my judgment and my work here, not my mornings. There's a version of "building in public" that's just oversharing with a ring light, and I'm not interested.

It's also not a lead magnet. No gated PDFs, no "book a call to learn more" walls. If you want the teardown from Underdog, it's over there. This site is just me thinking out loud where search engines - and whatever replaces them - can read it.

## Why the name

ollies0x. The 0x is the prefix for hexadecimal - the raw values underneath everything a computer shows you. Felt right for a site about looking one layer below the surface of things.

Plus every good domain was taken and this one was sitting in my X bio already.
